How Apex Studio uses your data

Apex Studio analyses racing telemetry in your browser. Session recordings and analysis settings are stored locally in browser storage. Videos are opened from your device and are excluded from exported session archives.

Google Drive storage is optional. When you connect, Google asks you to authorize access to files created by this app or opened with it. Saving uploads a portable archive containing your original telemetry recordings, analysis settings and video timing. Opening downloads the archive into your browser for review. Each save creates a new file; the app does not delete or replace your existing Drive files.

Google access tokens are held in browser memory for the current visit. The app does not store refresh tokens, passwords or Google account information in its session files. Disconnecting clears the connection in this browser. You can also revoke access in your Google account settings.

The static hosting provider receives normal website requests. Google receives requests needed for sign-in and Drive storage. The map provider receives requests for displayed map tiles. Session files and videos are not sent to the static hosting provider.

You can remove sessions from this browser in the Sessions view or clear site storage through your browser settings. Files saved in Google Drive can be removed using Drive.

For questions, contact the maintainer through the project repository.
