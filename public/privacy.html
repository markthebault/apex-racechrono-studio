How Apex Studio uses your data

Apex Studio analyses racing telemetry in your browser. Session recordings and analysis settings are stored locally in browser storage. Videos are opened from your device and are excluded from exported session archives.

Google Drive storage is optional. When you connect, Google asks you to authorize access to files created by this app or opened with it. Saving uploads a portable archive containing your original telemetry recordings, analysis settings and video timing. Opening downloads the archive into your browser for review. Save to Drive updates the current app-created session file. Save a new copy creates a separate file. You can choose a Drive folder or create one. Optional autosave sends changed telemetry, settings and video timing to your current file while this tab is open and connected. The app does not delete your Drive files.

Google access tokens are held in browser memory and this tab’s session storage until they expire, so a refresh can retain the connection. The app remembers a Drive account identifier and display name, chosen folder, current file, autosave preference and a list of saved file names in local browser storage. These are kept per Google account and are not included in exported session files. The app does not store refresh tokens or passwords. Disconnecting clears the access token and disables autosave; remembered file and folder details remain on this device. Clearing site storage removes those details. You can also revoke access in your Google account settings.

The static hosting provider receives normal website requests. Google receives requests needed for sign-in and Drive storage. The map provider receives requests for displayed map tiles. Session files and videos are not sent to the static hosting provider.

You can remove sessions from this browser in the Sessions view or clear site storage through your browser settings. Files saved in Google Drive can be removed using Drive.

For questions, contact the maintainer through the project repository.
